ENGINEERING UTILITY

Hydraulic Pipe Sizing & Flow Velocity Calculator

This professional engineering tool calculates water velocity and estimated pressure loss (head loss) based on pipe materials, sizes, and volumetric flow rates. High velocity triggers pipe erosion, while low velocity can cause particulate settlement.

High Erosion Hazard (> 2.5 m/s): Velocity too high. Promotes rapid friction erosion and dangerous water hammer pressures.
Optimal Flow Range (1.0 - 2.5 m/s): Perfectly balanced flow velocity for residential and commercial supply.
Low Velocity Sedimentation (< 1.0 m/s): Velocity too low. Risk of debris deposition and stagnation in gravity systems.
